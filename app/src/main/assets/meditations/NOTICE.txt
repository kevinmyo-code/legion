--- What was changed to make meditations.txt ---

Kept:    the twelve books and their 487 numbered sections, in Long's wording, including his square
         bracketed insertions such as "[the world]".
Removed: the Gutenberg header and footer; Long's biographical sketch, essay on the philosophy, the
         translator's footnotes (the [A], [B] ... blocks), both indexes, and the [Illustration]
         plates. Long's inline cross-reference parentheticals, e.g. "(iii. 7; vi. 23)", were removed
         because they are his editorial apparatus, not Marcus's words.
Changed: paragraphs that the original wrapped across lines were joined; underscores marking italics
         were dropped; the "+" marks Long used to flag a doubtful reading were removed; the transliterated Greek markers "[Greek: melos]" became "melos".
Added:   the "## BOOK" / "### SECTION" structure, so a passage can be cited as "Book IV, 3".
Section 1 of each book is unnumbered in the original and is numbered 1 here.
