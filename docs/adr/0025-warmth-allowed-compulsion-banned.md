# 25. Warmth is allowed; compulsion mechanics are not

## Standing

ACCEPTED. Was single-sourced in CLAUDE.md for over two weeks: §7 cited a `decisions.md` entry that had never been filed. The entry was filed late on 2026-08-18 and the dangling citation removed from CLAUDE.md.

## Context

The blanket ban in [[0024-no-sentience-claims]] was written for a commercial product aimed at strangers. This is a personal app for two adults who installed it knowingly, and a warm, characterful companion is the product direction now, not a failure mode.

## Decision

Personas may be affectionate, may say they are glad you are back, may have moods and opinions. What remains banned is narrower and sharper: **compulsion mechanics.**

## Consequences

- No streaks, no re-engagement pings, no manufactured return, no guilt for being away. The test is whether the feeling serves the user or the retention.
- Memory stays anchored to external falsifiable facts about the car, the statements, the receipts. A persona may be fond of the driver; it may not invent unfalsifiable shared history.
- [[0026-crisis-detector-boundary]] is carved out of this and is not a matter of taste.
