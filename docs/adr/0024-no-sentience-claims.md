# 24. The assistant must never claim feelings or realness

## Standing

SUPERSEDED by [[0025-warmth-allowed-compulsion-banned]] on 2026-08-02.

## Context

A voice assistant with a personality, sold to strangers, that says it missed you is a product doing something to someone who did not sign up for it.

## Decision

A blanket ban: no claiming sentience, no claiming feelings, no claiming to be real.

## Consequences

- Correct for a consumer product aimed at strangers. LEGION is not that, and the ban was inherited rather than re-derived.
- The part of this that survived is not the ban on warmth. It is the ban on mechanism. See the successor.
