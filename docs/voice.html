Talking to LEGION

LEGION is a voice assistant for the phone. Hold the button or say the wake word, then just talk. It looks after cars, money, food, training, notes and mail - and it will tell you when it cannot do something rather than guessing.

99 things it can do by voice.

Getting started

The first things worth trying.

“Give me a sitrep”
A status report: calendar, weather, car and a newsletter summary - whichever you have switched on.
“What time is it?”
The time and date where you are.
“Where am I?”
Your current location. Says it does not know rather than guessing when there is no GPS fix.
“Any severe weather nearby?”
Checks a live source for where you are now: severe weather (National Weather Service), earthquakes (USGS), wildfires (National Interagency Fire Center), or federal disaster declarations (FEMA). Always names its source, and needs a live GPS fix.
“What's the crime history around here?”
Reported offenses for the nearest police or sheriff's agency, from the FBI's Crime Data Explorer - agency-wide, roughly a year old, and voluntarily reported. It will not tell you whether somewhere is "safe"; nothing can answer that honestly from this data.
“Open the ledger”
Opens a screen in the app for you.
“I'm done setting up”
Finishes first-run setup.
“Go to sleep”
Stops listening and goes quiet. "That's all", "never mind" and "stand down" do the same. It reads this as YOU going dormant, not as your bedtime - no goodnight.

Your day

Calendar, lists, reminders and the round-up.

“Add milk to the shopping list”
Adds, ticks off, or changes an item on any of your lists.
“What's on my shopping list?”
Reads back a list.
“Remind me to call the shop at four”
Sets a reminder for a time, or for when you arrive somewhere.
“What's on today?”
Reads your Google Calendar. Says nothing is on when nothing is - it never invents an appointment.
“Save this as work”
Saves where you are now under a name, so reminders can trigger there.
“Forget the old gym”
Removes a saved place.
“Show my saved places”
Puts your saved places on screen.
“Navigate to the hardware store”
Hands off to your maps app.

The cars

Diagnostics, maintenance and history. Most of it needs the OBD dongle plugged in.

“Any trouble codes?”
Reads fault codes stored in the car right now.
“What's wrong with the car?”
Digs into the stored codes and explains them. Takes a few seconds.
“Clear the codes”
Clears stored fault codes. Asks you to confirm first.
“Has that code come back before?”
The history of faults this car has thrown.
“It's making a whining noise on turns”
Describe how the car is behaving and it works from that, not from a code.
“Is it ready for emissions?”
Whether the car's self-tests have run - the emissions readiness monitors.
“Did it do a cold start test?”
Whether the cold-start monitor has completed.
“What's the coolant temperature?”
Live readings from the OBD dongle.
“What's the oil temp?”
Reads one specific sensor by name.
“How's the car doing?”
An overall health summary - codes, temperatures, readiness.
“What am I getting to the gallon?”
Fuel economy from recorded drives.
“Is the voltage getting worse?”
How a reading has moved over time.
“What oil does it take?”
Factory specs for the car - engine, drivetrain, fluids.
“Look up this VIN”
Decodes a VIN into make, model and year.
“Any recalls on it?”
Open safety recalls, from the NHTSA database.
“What's due next?”
The next maintenance item, by miles or by date.
“When did I last do the brakes?”
Questions about your service history.
“I changed the oil today”
Records a service you just did.
“I did the plugs back in June”
Records a service from the past.
“Set the oil change to every 5000 miles”
Changes how often an item is due.
“The odometer reads 148,200”
Updates the mileage, which keeps due-dates honest.
“Log that I fitted new plugs, forty quid”
Records a build or modification, with cost.
“What have I done to this car?”
Reads back the build history.
“Add my Jeep”
Adds a car to the fleet.
“Register this vehicle”
Registers a vehicle by VIN or details.
“Rename the Jeep to the XJ”
Renames, updates or removes a car.
“What cars do I have?”
Lists your fleet.
“Ask the fleet advisor about my tyres”
Sends a harder car question to the fleet specialist.

Driving

Things that only make sense while you are actually out.

“Open the garage”
Triggers the garage relay. Asks you to confirm, and never claims to know whether the door opened or closed.
“Turn it up”
Changes the volume.

Money

Bank statements, budgets and spending. Figures come from your own imported statements.

“Import my bank statement”
Opens the statement importer.
“What's my balance?”
Balance from your imported statements.
“How much did I spend on fuel?”
Spending in a category or over a period.
“What did I spend last month?”
A month's total.
“What have I spent lately?”
Recent transactions.
“Sort out my uncategorised spending”
Sorts transactions into categories.
“That one's groceries”
Recategorises a transaction.
“Budget two hundred a month for fuel”
Sets a monthly budget for a category.
“What budgets do I have?”
Lists your budget categories.
“I just spent thirty on petrol”
Records a spend by voice before it hits the bank.
“What have I logged but not banked?”
Voice-logged spends the bank has not posted yet.
“That petrol one has gone through”
Clears a pending spend once the bank catches up.

Food and shopping

Groceries, receipts and meals.

“Scan this receipt”
Opens the camera to photograph a grocery receipt.
“Add eggs to the pantry”
Adds or updates something in the pantry.
“What did I buy last shop?”
Recent grocery purchases.
“How much on groceries this month?”
Grocery spending.
“I had chicken and rice”
Logs a meal. Calories and macros are estimates from the description, not measurements.
“What have I eaten today?”
Recent meals.
“When did I last eat?”
How long since your last logged meal.
“Set my protein target to 180”
Sets a daily nutrition target.
“Ask the pantry what I'm low on”
Sends a harder food question to the pantry specialist.

Training and sleep

Workouts, bodyweight and rest.

“Squats, five at a hundred kilos”

“What did I train this week?”
Recent workouts.
“When did I last train legs?”
How long since you trained.
“Make me a plan to get stronger”
Builds a training plan from a goal you describe.
“I'm eighty-two kilos”
Logs your bodyweight.
“I slept six hours, woke up twice”
Logs a night's sleep.
“How have I been sleeping?”
Recent sleep.

How long since your last sleep entry.
“I want eight hours a night”
Sets your nightly sleep target.
“Ask the coach about my recovery”
Sends a harder training or nutrition question to the coach.

Goals and advice

Longer-term things, and the per-aspect advisors.

“I want to save five grand by December”
Sets a goal.
“What am I working towards?”
Lists your current goals.
“I hit the savings goal”
Closes a goal as achieved or abandoned.
“How am I doing on my goals?”
Sends a goals question to the specialist.
“How am I doing overall?”
Asks one of the five advisors - body, notes, fleet, money, or overall.
“Yes, do that”
Accepts something an advisor proposed.
“Undo that”
Undoes the last thing you logged.

Mail and calendar

Needs a Google account connected.

“Any mail from the garage?”
Searches your Gmail. Mail is read and used, never stored.
“Read me that one”
Reads an email out.
“What did the insurance email say?”
Digs through your mail to answer a question.

Phone calls

Needs phone permissions granted in Setup.

“Answer it”
Picks up a ringing call. Add “and put it on speaker” and it does both. Cannot answer WhatsApp, Signal or Teams calls - Android does not allow it.
“Decline it”
Rejects a ringing call.

Memory

What it remembers about you, and why it said something.

“Remember that the XJ takes 5W-30”
Stores something for later.
“What do you remember about the Jeep?”
Looks up what it has stored.
“Why did you say that?”
Explains what triggered the last thing it said on its own - the rule and the fact behind it.

Settings and control

Changing how it behaves, by voice.

“Call yourself Alfred”
Renames your companion.
“Be drier about it”
Changes the personality.
“My name is Kevin”
Tells it what to call you.

You do not have to say these exactly. Ask in your own words - the phrases above are examples, not commands. If it cannot do something, it says so rather than pretending.

Some of these need a key or a permission. Music needs Spotify connected, mail and calendar need a Google account, caller ID needs phone permissions, and everything spoken needs a Gemini key. The Setup screen says which are missing.
