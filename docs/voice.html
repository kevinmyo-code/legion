Talking to LEGION

LEGION is a voice assistant for the phone. Tap the mic, or say "hey" and your companion's name if the wake phrase is switched on, then just talk. It waits for you to finish a thought rather than cutting in at the first pause, and on the phone's own speaker you can talk over it to stop it. Say "that will be all" when you are done. It looks after cars, money, food, training, notes and mail - and it will tell you when it cannot do something rather than guessing.

139 things it can do by voice.

Getting started

The first things worth trying.

“Give me my brief”
Your daily brief, built by your server at 5am: Canvas tasks due this week, money, the car, and tech news with its sources, then today's calendar and the weather from your phone, then a summary of your newsletters if you have them switched on. Ask "what's the news today?" for just the news. If the server can't be reached it reads the copy saved on your phone and says how old it is.By hand: The Brief button on the Home screen, beside the month name (and Brief in matrix mode's list) opens the same brief, with a Refresh button.
“What time is it?”
The time and date where you are.By hand: Voice only - your phone's own clock and status bar are the hands equivalent.
“Which way is north?”
Your current location, plus field figures worked out on the phone: compass heading and where true north is, MGRS grid, altitude, sunrise, sunset and turnaround time, the moon, and bearing and distance to your saved places ('bearing to the car'). Says it does not know rather than guessing when there is no GPS fix.By hand: The Field screen in the app drawer shows all of it with no signal; Fleet > Saved places shows the current location at the top.
“Any severe weather nearby?”
Checks a live source for where you are now: severe weather (National Weather Service), earthquakes (USGS), wildfires (National Interagency Fire Center), or federal disaster declarations (FEMA). Always names its source, and needs a live GPS fix.By hand: Home screen, area card (weather/quake/wildfire/disaster alerts and air quality).
“What's the crime history around here?”
Reported offenses for the nearest police or sheriff's agency, from the FBI's Crime Data Explorer - agency-wide, roughly a year old, and voluntarily reported. It will not tell you whether somewhere is "safe"; nothing can answer that honestly from this data.By hand: Voice only.
“Open the ledger”
Opens a screen in the app for you.By hand: Is itself navigation - every screen it can open is also reachable directly from the app's own tabs.
“I'm done setting up”
Finishes first-run setup.By hand: Onboarding screen's own final step.
“That will be all”
Stops listening and goes quiet. "That's all", "never mind" and "stand down" do the same. It reads this as YOU going dormant, not as your bedtime - no goodnight.By hand: Voice only - closing the app or its notification is the nearest hands equivalent.

“Show my saved places”
Puts your saved places on screen.By hand: Is itself the Fleet > Saved places screen.
“Show me my agenda”
Pops up today's due items - reminders, appointments, anything dated today - without leaving where you are.By hand: Calendar tab, today's day view shows the same thing directly.
“Show me my grocery spend by month”
Builds a one-off chart or total for a niche money question no screen already covers. It only ever picks what to look up - every number on screen comes from your own real data, never from a guess.By hand: Meters tab, the Ask section - pick source, aggregation, window and grouping by tapping.
“What does the engine say is due this week?”
Asks your household server directly for records the phone does not keep itself - what is due, checklists, places, body, ledger, pantry and fleet history - and can add or change them. It also checks conditions here or at a saved place from public agencies: river levels and flood stage, weather alerts, tropical storms, and road closures and fire detections once their keys are set. It also reads your GitHub repos when the server has a read-only token: did the build pass, what is open, recent commits. If the server is unreachable it says nothing was read or written, never that you have nothing.By hand: A dispatcher onto data your own screens already show (Calendar, checklists, Body, Ledger, Pantry, Fleet, places, recordings); two internal sync tables with no screen are refused. Conditions: Saved places, Conditions. GitHub status is on github.com and in the web assistant.

Driving

Things that only make sense while you are actually out.

“Take me to the hardware store”
Starts turn-by-turn directions inside LEGION, on its own map, with each turn spoken in one steady voice. It looks in your saved places, calendar and contacts first, then on the map. If several places could match it names the best one with its distance and waits for your yes before going. Say "show me the routes" to look without starting. It says so plainly when it could not find the place or no route came back.By hand: Fleet > Navigate: type the place, pick a route, tap Start. Every saved place also has a Navigate button.
“Add a stop at a gas station”
Changes the trip you are on: add or drop a stop, avoid tolls, highways or ferries, take the other route, mute or unmute the spoken turns (never the assistant itself), show the whole route or follow you again. If the new route cannot include the change it says the trip is unchanged.By hand: The navigation screen: the Stops, No tolls, Routes and Mute tiles and the overview button. Avoiding highways or ferries is voice only.
“How long until I get there?”
Reads the live trip: time left, distance left, arrival time, the next turn, the road you are on, the speed limit and traffic. With no trip it says it is not navigating, and a value the map does not have, like a speed limit on a road with none posted, it says it does not know.By hand: The navigation screen's turn banner and trip sheet show the same numbers.
“Stop navigating”
Ends the trip, or clears a route you were only previewing. Says so if nothing was running.By hand: The navigation screen's End button.
“Open the garage”
Triggers the garage relay. Asks you to confirm, and never claims to know whether the door opened or closed.By hand: Voice only - no garage button in the app.
“Turn it up”
Changes the volume.By hand: Media panel, volume control.

The web

LEGION's own browser. Sites you sign in to stay signed in; saved passwords come from Android's autofill.

“Find me a picture of a red XJ on stock height”
Opens LEGION's own browser on a Google search, Google Images, or a web address, and says it opened only once the page has loaded. Say 'read it' to hear the page and its numbered links and buttons, then 'tap 3', 'scroll down', 'type my email into 2', 'go back' or 'close the browser'. It never types a password: tap the field and Android's autofill offers the saved one. Before anything that pays, orders, sends, posts, books, deletes or submits a form it asks you first, and only a yes in your very next reply goes ahead. Feed sites like Instagram or YouTube are blocked the same way their apps are. To get an answer spoken rather than a page shown, just ask the question.By hand: Matrix home's Browser entry, or Browser in the app drawer: the address bar, back, forward, reload and close.

Claude session

Claude Code on your own laptops, for what the phone can't do. Nothing goes unless you name it or say yes.

“Use Claude on my Alienware to find the AW4 wiring PDF in my Downloads”
Sends a task to the Claude session on your Alienware or work laptop, but only when you name Claude or say yes to the offer; it offers when its own tools can't do something. It says whether the machine is on or, if it's off, since when, and that the task is queued. It never calls a task done when it was only sent. Ask what your Claude session is doing to hear each machine, the running tasks and their current step, the results, and the open Claude Code sessions, the ones waiting on you first. When a task finishes you hear the result (or get a notification), and if it asks something it listens a few seconds for your yes; a file it found is named with its size and only comes to your phone, into Downloads, if you say yes. Ask for files by name ("find the scanned form on my PC and send it to my phone") and they come straight to the phone and open there, with Share for WhatsApp or anything else; the engine deletes its copy once the phone has it. More than 20 files or 100 MB, or not what you named, and it asks first. Answer its question with a yes, or follow up ("now email it to me"). Name one of the workflows on a laptop ("have Claude run the weekly digest") and it starts that one; ask what your Claude session is doing to hear each machine's workflows, and what one does. A workflow that runs on its own schedule stops at every question and asks you first, as a notification or out loud. Ask for research written up ("make me an itinerary for a day trip into Atlanta") and it offers to have the Alienware make it as a page: when it's ready it asks "want me to open it?" and a yes opens it in LEGION's browser. The work laptop sends summaries only, never files. "Stop telling me about Claude tasks" turns the announcements off; "tell me about Claude tasks again" turns them back on.By hand: Home, More, Claude session: machines, send a task, answer a question, Send to phone, the interactive sessions, and Open page on a finished task, Files to my phone in the send box and Open files on a task that brought some, Workflows with their last run and a Run button, scheduled tasks labelled Scheduled, the switch for announcements (also Settings, Proactive speech, Claude tasks); tapping a page's or files' notification opens it.

LEGION itself

Updates to the app, published by you and installed only on your yes.

“Is there an update for LEGION?”
Checks whether a newer LEGION has been published, and says what it changes. The update downloads on Wi-Fi only (unless you allow mobile data) and is checked first: a file whose checksum or signing key does not match the app on your phone is thrown away, and it says so. It always asks before installing, and only your yes installs it. Asked during a conversation, it installs as soon as the conversation ends; in Matrix mode or with the car connected it won't install at all, and says why. LEGION closes for about a minute and comes back on its own. When an update is ready you get a notification with Install and Later, and Alfred mentions it once at a quiet moment.By hand: Settings, Updates: this phone's version, the newest one and its notes, Check now, Install, Allow mobile data, the one-time Allow installs grant, and which wording Alfred is running; or Install on the update's notification.

Always reads the target back first (the name, or the digits) and waits for a yes before dialling, so a misheard digit never dials a stranger. Asks rather than guesses if a name matches nobody or several people. Refuses emergency numbers outright and tells you to dial them yourself. Cannot send texts - that was ruled out on purpose.By hand: Settings > Phone dial screen.

Recordings

“Record this meeting”

“Stop recording”

Memory

What it remembers about you, and why it said something.

“Remember that the XJ takes 5W-30 / What do you remember about the Jeep? / Forget that”
Saves, looks up, lists and forgets what it remembers about you. Memories live on your engine, private to you unless you say the household may know one, and the web desk sees the same ones. Forget deletes it for good. With no connection it answers from the phone's last copy and says so, and it won't save or forget anything until it's back online. ID numbers are refused and pointed to the ID vault.By hand: Settings > Data and privacy > Memory: search, add, edit, share or make private, and Forget (asks first, deletes for good).
“Show my passport number”

Copy puts it on the clipboard for 60 seconds.By hand: Settings > Data and privacy > ID vault: add, edit, show and delete items behind your fingerprint.
