# Ideas: making Alfred feel more like a person
Saved Nov 25, 2026 from a voice call with Kevin. IDEAS ONLY, not tickets and not committed work (feature freeze: ship the voice demo first).

Kevin's goal, in his words: "the goal here is, like, not an AI system... I want to create, like, an actual sentient program." He already mimics human memory with memory decay.

Human things worth borrowing:
1. **Sleep:** an overnight pass where Alfred consolidates the day, keeps what mattered and lets the rest fade.
2. **Emotional weight:** things Kevin was excited or stressed about stick longer, the way they do for people.
3. **Curiosity:** Alfred follows up later on his own, like "how'd the interview go?"
4. **Moods and energy:** Alfred matches Kevin's state, quieter when he's tired.
5. **Opinions:** Alfred sometimes pushes back instead of always agreeing.
