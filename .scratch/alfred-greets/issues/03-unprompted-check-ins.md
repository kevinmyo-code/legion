# Unprompted check-ins

Morning first unlock; 30 to 60 minutes after an event ends; arriving somewhere new; something due
within a day. Each is a registered raise declaring its anchor and its silence instruction
(compulsion test, check-ins exempt from (b) only). No daily cap; quiet hours setting (default 10pm
to 8am). A topic answered or silenced is not raised again. Per-topic silence (body, money, place,
coursework, events) in one instruction or one setting.
