# Does Mapbox's vehicle-usage clause cover a phone app

## Question

Product Terms 1.2.2 (dated 2026-07-21) require a separate paid licence for an application
"related to vehicle usage", including "applications primarily intended for use within vehicles".
Does a phone assistant with a navigation feature (and an OBD fleet aspect) fall under it?

Mapbox sells the Nav SDK for Mobile self-serve per trip, which suggests not. The text does not say
so. **One email to Mapbox settles it in writing; a "yes, buy a licence" ends this map.**

Detail: [research/01-sdk-facts.md](../research/01-sdk-facts.md) section 6.

## Answer (2026-10-03)

**Kevin's ruling:** *"covers a phone app. im not gonna email mapbox man its just a hobby app for my
self."* LEGION is a personal app with no commercial model (§2), and Kevin reads 1.2.2 as aimed at
in-vehicle systems, not a phone feature. The clause's text stays ambiguous; the risk is accepted
knowingly, not resolved. If Mapbox ever says otherwise, this is the ticket to reopen.
