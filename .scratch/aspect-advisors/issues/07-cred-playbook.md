# Research: the CRED finance playbook

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

Assemble the personal-finance framework the CRED advisor ships with: budget-ratio heuristics
(50/30/20 and its critiques), emergency-fund sizing, debt-payoff ordering (avalanche vs
snowball), savings-rate and long-term-goal tracking math (how "on track for $X by YYYY" is
judged), and spending-review heuristics (category creep, subscription audit). US context only
(the SG entity is out of scope per legion-shape). Everything must be phrasable as general
best practice and estimate, never as professional financial advice - flag where that boundary
bites. Sources licensing-clean to paraphrase (government financial-literacy material, widely
published frameworks). Deliverable: distilled playbook draft in `research/cred-playbook.md`
sized for a SubAgent brief, with a sources list.

## Answer

Playbook drafted: `research/cred-playbook.md`. Spine is MyMoney.gov's five principles
(Earn/Save/Protect/Spend/Borrow); 50/30/20 as a diagnostic lens with CFPB's own sustainability
caveat, 28/36 debt-load ceilings, 3-6 months essential-expense emergency fund (CFPB fixes no
dollar figure), avalanche-default/snowball-when-stalled debt ordering, 15%-of-gross retirement
rate plus Fidelity salary multiples (assumptions named), goal tracking as app-computed
future-value projections the advisor only interprets, and category-creep / subscription-audit
heuristics. Hard referral boundaries flagged for tax, investment selection, insurance, debt
restructuring. Government sources are public domain; commercial ones used only for
uncopyrightable rules of thumb.

Assumptions ledger: 50/30/20 CFPB usage, MyMoney five, 3-6 month fund, avalanche-vs-snowball
(incl. Journal of Consumer Research completion finding), 15% rate and multiples with their
assumptions, 28/36, subscription-audit heuristics - **researched** (web, 2026-08-13). Answer
shape (section 8), digest division of labor, referral boundaries, 10-15% creep threshold -
**reasoned** from map decisions and CLAUDE.md §7, not sourced.
