# Ticket 20 phase 2: offline sweep on Kevin's enrollment (2026-11-03)

Numbers only. No audio, no transcripts beyond the wake phrase. Source: `tools/wake_eval/` (core.replay,
engines.SherpaKws / SherpaVad / VoskConfirm) on enrollment `enroll-20261103-103441-214`, A25,
VOICE_COMMUNICATION, effects=2, two-stage on. sherpa-onnx 1.13.8 + vosk (pip), model files from
`app/src/main/assets/wake-kws` and `vosk-model`. Recordings deleted after the run.

## Data

- 20 positive takes (6 normal, 4 quiet, 4 far, 3 fast, 3 music), 4 s each.
- **0 negative takes.** `neg_ambient_01` (the 2-minute negative) was never recorded: logcat stops
  after `pos_music_03`, and no `neg_*` file exists on the phone.
- **False accepts: not measured (0 s of negative audio).** Every ranking below is recall-only.
  `Score.meets()` is False for every point.
- `live/`: 1 segment, unlabelled, skipped (harness rule).

## Harness deviations (reproduce with these)

The stock CLI refuses to run with zero negatives; a scratch wrapper called the same core/engines
functions with two changes:

1. Fixed input-gain axis: int16 PCM multiplied by g and clipped to [-32768, 32767] before the
   VAD/KWS/ring (stand-in for a gain stage ahead of `WakeTwoStageDetector.process`).
2. One `SherpaVad` reused per run with `reset()` per take, one gain per process (the machine ran
   out of commit memory creating a VAD per replay).

## Input level (the first finding)

Speech RMS (95th percentile of 20 ms frames) per take: **-51 to -74 dBFS**; peaks -22 to -63 dBFS.
Close-mic speech is normally around -20 to -30 dBFS RMS, so the capture arrives ~30 dB low.

Loud takes also missed on the phone: `pos_music_01` (peak -34, RMS -57) and `pos_music_02`
(peak -39, RMS -58) were kws_miss; `pos_fast_02` peak -22 is a transient (RMS -59), kws_miss.
`pos_quiet_01` (RMS -64) opened. Within the recorded range, level alone does not separate hits
from misses. But the whole range sits far below normal speech level.

## Replay fidelity (shipped point, gain 1)

- Replay 4/20 opened, phone 4/20. Per-take open agreement 18/20 (`pos_normal_02` replay-only,
  `pos_quiet_01` phone-only).
- Stage level differs: replay shows no_vad on 7 takes where the phone saw vad_on. The phone VAD runs
  warm across takes; the replay starts it cold per 4 s WAV. Treat kws_miss vs no_vad splits as soft.
- n = 20: one take = 5 points of recall.

## Verdict

- No point meets the target (>= 95% recall, <= 1 FA/h). Best recall 70% (74% excluding the
  near-silent normal_01), FA unmeasured.
- Per the ticket rule, nothing ships as "tuned" on this data. The 14/20 point is a candidate,
