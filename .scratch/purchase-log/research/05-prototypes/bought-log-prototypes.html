Purchase-log ticket 05. Mia's iPhone family view first (390 x 844, soft Material light, the real web tokens). Then Kevin's workbench (variant A) and Kevin's Android phone (variant C, 384 dp, soft Material dark). Everything is clickable: search, tick a Groceries line, untick it, log something by hand, open an entry. The chips above each device jump to a state.

Mia's iPhone mocks are signed in as Mia, so she sees her own private entry and not Kevin's. The workbench and Android are Kevin, so the reverse. Prices are what someone typed and every one says so.
