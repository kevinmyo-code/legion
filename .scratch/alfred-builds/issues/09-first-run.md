# Kevin's first run: a fix, Snake, a horror book

1. **A fix.** "Alfred, that was wrong" after a real bad answer. Expect a fix with a test, "Ship it?", then yes, then the update or deploy, then the behaviour is fixed.
2. **A feature.** "Build me Snake in my games." Expect at most 2 questions, then the build, then "Ship it?", then yes, then "Install?", then Snake in Games.
3. **Content.** "Recommend me something horror", pick one, "find it". Expect a legal free EPUB in Books, or an honest "no free copy, here's where to borrow it".
4. **Undo.** "Undo the last update" restores the previous build or revision.
